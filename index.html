📌 TL;DR

VISD is a structured self-distillation framework that improves video reasoning by turning diagnostic judge feedback into dense token-level supervision, while reinforcement rewards keep policy updates aligned with task objectives.

Abstract

Training VideoLLMs for complex reasoning remains challenging due to sparse sequence-level rewards and the lack of fine-grained credit assignment over long, temporally grounded reasoning trajectories. VISD introduces structured privileged information for video reasoning: a video-aware judge decomposes reasoning quality into answer correctness, logical consistency, and spatio-temporal grounding, then uses this feedback to guide teacher-based token-level supervision.

Motivation

Video reasoning needs models to identify not only whether an answer is correct, but also where and why the reasoning succeeds or fails. Sparse RLVR rewards provide reliable final supervision, but they do not diagnose temporal misalignment, spatial grounding mistakes, or inconsistent intermediate reasoning.

Method Overview

VISD augments on-policy video reasoning training with structured privileged feedback. A judge model evaluates each rollout, a feedback-conditioned teacher replays the same completion, and direction-magnitude decoupling combines sparse reward direction with dense token-level update magnitudes.
